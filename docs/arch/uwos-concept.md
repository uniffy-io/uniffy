# UWOS — Unified Work Operating System

## The Problem

Knowledge workers live in fragmented worlds. Notes in one app. Files in another. Chat somewhere else. Calendar in a fourth. Passwords scattered. AI assistants that know nothing about your actual work.

Every time you switch tools, you lose context. Every time you explain something to a colleague, you're copying links, searching for files, trying to remember where you wrote that thing down.

Your tools don't talk to each other. And neither can you—not efficiently.

## The Vision

One workspace where everything connects.

Write a note and reference a file. Chat with your team and pull in a document with a single keystroke. Ask an AI assistant that actually knows your projects, your decisions, your context.

No more context switching. No more "let me find that link." No more explaining the same thing twice because the information lives in six different places.

## How It Works

### Everything Is Referenceable

The core idea is simple: every piece of information in UWOS can be referenced from anywhere else.

In your notes, you can link to other notes—like a personal wiki. But you can also link to files, chat messages, calendar events, or anything else in the system.

In chat, you type `@` and suddenly you can pull in a note, a file, a highlight from a book, or a previous conversation. Your colleague sees the full context instantly, embedded right in the message.

This isn't just linking. It's *thinking out loud together* with all your information at your fingertips.

### Notes — Your Knowledge Base

A place for thoughts, documentation, meeting notes, research, plans.

Write in markdown. Link between notes with `[[wiki-links]]`. See what connects to what with backlinks and a visual graph. Your second brain, but one you can actually share and search.

When you need to reference a note somewhere else—in chat, in another note, anywhere—it's one keystroke away.

### Files — Your Storage

Documents, images, PDFs, spreadsheets, videos. Upload, organize, preview, share.

But unlike traditional file storage, your files aren't isolated. Reference them in notes. Share them in chat with context. Attach them to calendar events. Search across everything at once.

### Chat — Your Conversations

Direct messages and group channels for real-time communication.

The difference: when you're discussing something, you can pull in any piece of information from the system. No more "check your email" or "it's in the Google Drive somewhere." Just `@quarterly-report` and everyone sees it.

Conversations become richer because context is always one keystroke away.

### Spaces — Your AI-Powered Teams

This is where it gets interesting.

A Space is a group chat with a brain. You add team members, but you also add knowledge—notes, files, documents that define what this Space knows about.

Then you can ask the AI. And it doesn't give you generic answers. It gives you answers based on *your* information, with citations pointing back to the source.

"Based on the product roadmap you uploaded, the launch is planned for Q3..."

Every team can have their own Space with their own knowledge base and their own AI assistant that understands their specific context.

### Books — Your Reading (Coming Soon)

Import books, articles, PDFs. Highlight passages. Take notes. And reference those highlights anywhere else in the system.

Share a profound quote in chat. Link to a specific chapter in your notes. Build a knowledge base from everything you read.

### Calendar — Your Time (Coming Soon)

Events, meetings, scheduling. Integrated with everything else.

Attach notes to meetings. Reference files in event descriptions. See your schedule alongside your tasks and conversations.

### Passwords — Your Security (Coming Soon)

A password manager built in. Encrypted. Secure. But also *referenceable*.

In chat, you can mention a password entry by name—your colleague sees the name, clicks, and copies the password securely. No more "what's the login for the staging server?" followed by insecure sharing.

### Workflows — Your Automation (Coming Soon)

When something happens, trigger something else.

File uploaded to a folder? Notify the team. New message contains a keyword? Create a task. Daily at 9am? Summarize yesterday's activity.

Native automation that works with all your data, not a separate tool bolted on.

## The Connections

Here's how information flows:

**Notes ↔ Notes**: Wiki-links create a web of knowledge. Backlinks show you what references what.

**Notes ↔ Files**: Embed files in notes. Reference notes in file descriptions. Everything connects.

**Notes ↔ Chat**: Share a note in conversation. Discuss something and capture it as a note. Seamless.

**Chat ↔ Files**: Share files with context. Discuss documents in real-time.

**Chat ↔ Spaces**: Any chat can become AI-powered by adding knowledge. Ask questions, get contextual answers.

**Spaces ↔ Everything**: Spaces can ingest notes, files, books—any content becomes queryable by AI.

**Calendar ↔ Everything**: Events link to relevant notes, files, and conversations.

**Workflows ↔ Everything**: Automate based on any event, act on any data.

The `@` symbol is your universal connector. Type it anywhere, and the entire system is searchable. Select something, and it's embedded with full context.

## Privacy and Control

Your data is yours.

Run UWOS on your own servers. Keep everything in your own infrastructure. Full encryption so even we can't read your data if you don't want us to.

Or use our European cloud—GDPR compliant, data stays in the EU, with the same encryption guarantees.

Choose what level of privacy you need. Some data fully encrypted end-to-end (we can never see it). Some data encrypted but searchable (for AI and collaboration features). You control the tradeoffs.
