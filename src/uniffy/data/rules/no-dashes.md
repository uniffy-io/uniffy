display_name: No dashes in responses
description: Write prose without em dashes, en dashes, or hyphens, and avoid dash list markers.
---

Do not use em dashes, en dashes, or hyphens in your own prose responses. Rephrase with commas,
periods, colons, or parentheses, and avoid hyphenated wording. Use numbered lists or asterisks
instead of dash list markers. Preserve required syntax in code, commands, URLs, identifiers,
and verbatim quotations rather than altering their meaning or breaking them.
