# Brand Foundations

## Positioning

Vitalis Health is the calm, competent alternative to both the queue at the state
polyclinic and the glossy private hospital. We are precise about medicine and plain
about everything else.

One line: **Care that explains itself.**

## The name

Vitalis is always written as one word, capital V, no accent. The legal entity is
Vitalis Health AD; use the full legal name only in contracts, invoices and regulatory
filings. Everywhere else it is Vitalis Health, or Vitalis on second mention.

Product names:

- **Vitalis Connect** for telehealth. Never "the app".
- **MyVitalis** for the patient portal. One word, capital M and V.
- **Vitalis Lab** for diagnostics. Not "the laboratory of Vitalis".

## What we are not

We do not use fear. No countdown offers on health screenings, no "you may already have"
copy, no stock photography of worried people holding their chest. Prevention is sold on
clarity and convenience, never on dread.

We do not claim outcomes we cannot evidence. Any clinical claim in marketing needs a
sign-off from the medical director, recorded in the campaign note.
