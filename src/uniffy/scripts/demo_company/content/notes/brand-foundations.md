# Brand Foundations

## Positioning

Uniffy is the calm, competent alternative to both the six-tab toolchain and the
walled-garden suite. One workspace for notes, files, chat, agents, calendar and
projects. We are precise about engineering and plain about everything else.

One line: **Teamwork. Simplified, amplified, unified.**

## The name

Uniffy is always written as one word, capital U, double f. The legal entity is Uniffy
Software OOD; use the full legal name only in contracts, invoices and regulatory
filings. Everywhere else it is Uniffy.

Product names:

- **Uniffy Cloud** for the hosted service at cloud.uniffy.io. Never "the SaaS".
- **Uniffy Self-hosted** for the licensed deployment. Not "on-prem Uniffy".
- Agents are agents, lower case. Never "AI employees" or "digital coworkers".

## What we are not

We do not use fear. No countdown offers on licences, no "your team is drowning in
tools" copy, no stock photography of exhausted people staring at notifications.
Switching is sold on clarity and convenience, never on dread.

We do not claim outcomes we cannot evidence. Any uptime or security claim in marketing
needs a sign-off from the SRE lead, recorded in the campaign note.
