# Expenses and Travel

Owner: Finance. Claims are submitted monthly, with receipts, and paid with the next
payroll run.

## Principle

Spend the company's money as carefully as your own, and never let a cost decision touch
a clinical one. If a cheaper option adds clinical risk, take the safer option and write
one line explaining why.

## Approval thresholds

| Amount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 200 BGN | Self, with receipt |
| 200 to 2000 BGN | Line manager |
| 2000 to 5000 BGN | Department head |
| Above 5000 BGN | Management team, minuted |

Clinical consumables and lab reagents are outside this ladder and follow the procurement
process run by the lab manager, per [Lab Sample Handling](lab-sample-handling-sop.md).

## Travel

Sofia to Plovdiv is a company car or train, second class. Book at least a week ahead
where possible. Taxis are for early clinic starts, late finishes, or moving equipment.

International travel is economy class. Hotels up to 180 EUR per night in Western Europe
and 120 EUR elsewhere. Anything above needs department head approval before booking.

## Meals

Per diem for travel days: 40 BGN domestic, 60 EUR international. Client and partner
meals need the names of attendees on the claim. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Not reimbursed

Parking fines, personal fitness, personal phone contracts, home furniture, and travel
upgrades bought at the airport.

## Conferences

CPD budget covers the ticket and the travel. Anything presented on behalf of Vitalis is
reviewed by the medical director for clinical claims, per
[Brand Foundations](brand-foundations.md).
