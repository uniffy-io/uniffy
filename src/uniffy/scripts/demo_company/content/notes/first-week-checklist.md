# First Week Checklist

Your manager owns this list with you. Everything here should be done by Friday of week
one; if something is blocked, say so on the Wednesday, not the Friday.

The same list, in spreadsheet form for managers, is
[onboarding-checklist.csv](file:People/onboarding-checklist.csv).

## Day one

- [ ] [New joiner welcome](event:New joiner welcome) session at 10:00
- [ ] Badge and photo at reception, floors set for your role
- [ ] Laptop, password manager, multi-factor enrolment
- [ ] Read [Company Overview](company-overview.md) and [Mission and Values](mission-and-values.md)
- [ ] Read [Information Security Policy](information-security-policy.md) and confirm it
- [ ] Tour of the floor, fire exits, assembly point, first aid kit
- [ ] Lunch with the team

## Day two and three

- [ ] Accounts for the systems your role needs, per [Systems and Access](systems-and-access.md)
- [ ] Read [How We Work](how-we-work.md) and [Remote Work Policy](remote-work-policy.md)
- [ ] Meet your buddy, book a standing 1:1 for the first month
- [ ] Payroll, bank details and contract paperwork with People
- [ ] Book your first 1:1 with your manager

## Support roles only

- [ ] Desk account and headset set up, added to the #support channel
- [ ] [Customer Onboarding Runbook](customer-onboarding-runbook.md) and
      [Support Escalation Runbook](support-escalation-runbook.md) read and acknowledged
- [ ] Shadow two desk shifts before answering tickets alone
- [ ] Ticket system training, signed off by the support lead
- [ ] Call induction if your rota includes customer calls,
      see [Support Escalation Runbook](support-escalation-runbook.md)

## Engineering roles only

- [ ] Repository, CI and staging access provisioned
- [ ] [Release and Deploy Runbook](release-and-deploy-runbook.md) read and acknowledged
- [ ] First supervised deploy scheduled with the SRE lead

## By the end of week one

- [ ] [GDPR and customer data refresher](event:GDPR and customer data refresher) booked
- [ ] Data protection training completed, recorded by the DPO
- [ ] Calendar reflects your working pattern
- [ ] You know who to call out of hours, and they know you
- [ ] One thing about this checklist that confused you, sent to People. Every intake
      improves it.
