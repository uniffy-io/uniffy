# Voice and Tone

## The voice

Plain, warm, exact. We write the way a good doctor speaks at the end of a consultation:
short sentences, no jargon unless the jargon is the point, and always a next step.

Bulgarian and English versions are both first-class. Neither is a translation of the
other; both are written from the same brief so each reads naturally.

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone |
| --- | --- |
| Appointment reminder | Brief, practical, one action |
| Normal result | Warm, reassuring, no drama |
| Abnormal result | Calm, specific, always with a booked next step |
| Billing | Neutral, itemised, no euphemism |
| Service incident | Direct, what happened, what we are doing, when we will update |

## Rules that survive review

- Say the number. "Your vitamin D is 18 ng/mL, below the 30 we look for" beats "slightly low".
- Never deliver a bad result without a route to a human. Every abnormal result message
  carries a booking link and the clinic phone number.
- No diminutives for procedures. It is a blood draw, not a little prick.
- Avoid "simply", "just", "obviously". If it were obvious the patient would not be reading.
- Times and dates in Bulgarian format for patients: 14:30, 05.03.2026.
- Money in BGN, with EUR in brackets when the audience is international.

## Examples

Weak: "Unfortunately your appointment has been cancelled due to unforeseen circumstances."

Better: "Dr Ilieva is off sick today, so your 14:30 appointment is cancelled. The next
free slot with her is Thursday 09:15, or you can see Dr Marinov today at 16:00. Reply or
call 0700 10 220 and we will book it."

Clinical accuracy still outranks tone. When the two conflict, ask the medical director.
Communication that carries a clinical claim follows [Brand Foundations](brand-foundations.md).
