# Plovdiv Office Guide

Trakia district, Plovdiv. Opened in 2021, home to the support pod, the QA pod, the
device lab and a small administrative office.

## Hours and cover

Weekdays 09:00 to 18:00, with the support pod covering the late desk shift until 20:00
on a rota. Two support engineers and three QA engineers work here full time; the desk
rota is published two weeks ahead by the support lead.

Cover rotates from Sofia: a platform engineer visits on Tuesdays and the monthly
[QA regression day](event:QA regression day, Plovdiv) fills the office on the first
Thursday.

## The device lab

Plovdiv runs the device lab: racked test phones, tablets and laptops covering the
browsers and OS versions we support. The QA pod runs the regression suite against each
candidate build; results gate the rollout per
[Release and Deploy Runbook](release-and-deploy-runbook.md). A device is couriered to
Sofia on demand when an engineer needs to reproduce a hardware-specific bug, with the
QA lead's approval, and comes back on the next run.

## Rooms

[Maritsa](room:Maritsa) seats six and is bookable in the workspace calendar.
[Trakia Training Room](room:Trakia Training Room) seats sixteen and doubles as the
community space: [Community webinar evening](event:Community webinar evening)
blocks it every Wednesday from 17:00.

## Local practicalities

Parking is free in the office's own lot, twelve spaces. Bus 26 stops in front of the
building. The nearest cafe with decent espresso is in the same block and stays open
until 21:00.

Staff travelling from Sofia should book the company car through the office manager; the
drive is roughly two hours each way, so day trips are planned around the 09:30 team
leads sync rather than on top of it. Travel costs follow
[Expenses and Travel](expenses-and-travel-policy.md).

## Escalation

Production escalation goes to the on-call Sofia engineer. Building, power or network
failures go to the Plovdiv office manager, who has authority to close the office and
move the pod to remote work without waiting for Sofia.
