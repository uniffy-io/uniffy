# Plovdiv Clinic Guide

Trakia district, Plovdiv. Opened in 2021, nine consulting rooms, a sample collection
point and a small administrative office.

## Hours and cover

Weekdays 07:30 to 19:30, Saturdays 08:00 to 14:00. Two GPs, one paediatrician and a
rotating specialist run the weekday rota. The Saturday rota is one GP and one nurse.

Specialist cover rotates from Sofia: cardiology on Tuesdays, endocrinology on Thursdays.
The rota is published two weeks ahead by the Plovdiv clinic manager.

## Samples

Plovdiv does not run its own analysers. Samples are collected until 11:00 and go by
courier to [Vitalis Lab](lab-sample-handling-sop.md) in Sofia at 11:30. Anything drawn
after the courier leaves is stored per the cold chain rules and goes the next morning,
except urgent panels, which are couriered on demand with the clinic manager's approval.

## Rooms

[Maritsa](room:Maritsa) seats six and is bookable in the workspace calendar.
[Trakia Training Room](room:Trakia Training Room) seats sixteen and doubles as the
patient education space: [Patient education evening](event:Patient education evening)
blocks it every Wednesday from 17:00.

## Local practicalities

Parking is free in the clinic's own lot, twelve spaces. Bus 26 stops in front of the
building. The nearest pharmacy is in the same block and stays open until 21:00.

Staff travelling from Sofia should book the company car through the office manager; the
drive is roughly two hours each way, so day trips are planned around the 09:30 clinic
managers sync rather than on top of it. Travel costs follow
[Expenses and Travel](expenses-and-travel-policy.md).

## Escalation

Clinical escalation goes to the on-call Sofia specialist. Building, power or equipment
failures go to the Plovdiv clinic manager, who has authority to close a room and
reschedule patients without waiting for Sofia.
