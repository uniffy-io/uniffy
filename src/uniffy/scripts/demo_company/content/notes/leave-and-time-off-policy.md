# Leave and Time Off

Owner: Head of People. Bulgarian Labour Code sets the floor; everything below is at or
above it.

## Annual leave

25 working days per year for everyone, five above the statutory minimum. Leave accrues
monthly and carries over until 30 June of the following year.

Requests go through the workspace calendar and need manager approval. Support engineers
request at least four weeks ahead so the desk rota can absorb it; two people on the
same desk shift cannot be off in the same week without the support lead's sign-off.

August and the last week of December are heavily booked. First requested, first approved.

## Sick leave

Report to your manager before the start of your shift, or as early as possible.
Bulgarian sick notes are filed with People within three working days. Nobody is expected
to work while ill, and nobody carries an on-call rotation while ill; the swap is
arranged the same day.

## Parental leave

Statutory maternity, paternity and parental leave apply in full. On top of that Uniffy
adds ten paid days for the second parent, to be taken in the first six months. Return
from parental leave includes a phased first month at 80 percent load on full pay.

## Other paid leave

| Reason | Days |
| --- | --- |
| Marriage | 2 |
| Bereavement, immediate family | 3, extended on request |
| Blood donation | 1, plus the day of donation |
| Moving home | 1 per year |
| Study leave for accredited courses | Up to 5 per year |

## Learning and development

Everyone gets a yearly learning budget and the study days above. Conference travel
follows [Expenses and Travel](expenses-and-travel-policy.md).

## Public holidays

Bulgarian public holidays are observed. The support desk runs a reduced holiday rota on
holiday weekends; the rota is published a month ahead.
