# Telehealth Consultation SOP

Controlled document. Owner: Medical Director. Covers every consultation delivered
through Vitalis Connect.

## Before the consultation

- Confirm the environment meets [Remote Work Policy](remote-work-policy.md): private
  room, headset, stable connection.
- Review the record: last contact, medication, allergies, outstanding results.
- Confirm the patient's identity with two identifiers at the start of the call.
- Confirm the patient is somewhere they can speak freely. If someone else is in the
  room, ask whether the patient wants them there.

## During

State that the consultation is not recorded and that a clinical note will be written.
Camera on for both sides unless the patient declines; a declined camera is recorded in
the note along with the reason.

Get an address. If the patient deteriorates mid-call you need to know where to send help.

## What telehealth cannot do

Escalate to a face-to-face appointment or emergency services for: chest pain, focal
neurology, breathlessness at rest, abdominal pain needing examination, suspected
fracture, anything needing auscultation to decide, and any child under one year with a
fever. When in doubt, see the patient.

## After

- Clinical note written within the same session, before the next patient.
- Prescriptions issued electronically; controlled substances are never prescribed in a
  first telehealth contact.
- Results, referrals and instructions published to MyVitalis in plain language, per
  [Voice and Tone](voice-and-tone.md).
- Follow-up booked before the call ends when one is needed. "Call us if it gets worse"
  is not a follow-up plan on its own.

## Technical failure

If the connection fails twice, switch to a phone call and note it. If the clinical
question needs vision, rebook rather than guess. Repeated platform failures are logged
with IT the same day; a pattern of failures is a patient safety issue, not an IT
annoyance.
