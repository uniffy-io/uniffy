# Adverse Event Reporting

Controlled document. Owner: Medical Director with the QA Lead. Everyone can report;
nobody needs permission from a manager to do it.

## What to report

- Any harm, or near harm, to a patient.
- Medication errors, including ones caught before administration.
- Wrong patient, wrong sample, wrong result, wrong room.
- Delayed or lost results, including delays caused by systems.
- Equipment failures with clinical consequence.
- Aggression or violence towards staff or patients.

Near misses count. A near miss is a free lesson; a pattern of unreported near misses is
how a serious incident gets built.

## How to report

Report the same day, in the workspace, using the incident form. Include what happened,
when, who was involved, what the immediate consequence was, and what was done
straight away. Facts only in the report; interpretation goes in the review.

Do not put the patient's full record in the report. Reference the record instead, as
required by [Patient Data Protection Policy](patient-data-protection-policy.md).

## What happens next

| Severity | Response |
| --- | --- |
| No harm, no near miss | Logged, reviewed monthly |
| Near miss | Reviewed within 5 working days |
| Harm, recovered | Review within 48 hours, patient informed |
| Serious harm or death | Immediate escalation to the medical director and management |

Serious incidents are reported to the Executive Agency for Medical Supervision where the
regulation requires it, and to the insurer. The DPO is involved whenever patient data
was part of the failure.

## Being open with patients

Patients affected by an incident are told, in person where possible, what happened and
what we are changing. We apologise plainly. An apology is not an admission of liability
under Bulgarian law, and withholding one is both unkind and bad practice.

## Just culture

Reviews look for the conditions that made the error likely: staffing, interface design,
interruptions, ambiguous protocols. Individuals are named only where there is wilful
misconduct. This is the promise that keeps reports coming in, and it is stated in
[Mission and Values](mission-and-values.md).
