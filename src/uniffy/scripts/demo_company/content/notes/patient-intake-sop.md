# Patient Intake SOP

Controlled document. Owner: Medical Director. Approved by the patient safety committee.
Changes require committee approval, per [How We Work](how-we-work.md).

## Scope

Every first contact with a patient at Sofia HQ and Plovdiv, walk-in or booked.

## Steps

1. **Identify.** Full name, date of birth, personal identification number. Two
   identifiers must match the booking before anything clinical happens.
2. **Verify coverage.** NHIF status, private insurance, or self-pay. Coverage is never a
   reason to delay urgent assessment; it is settled after.
3. **Consent and privacy notice.** The patient receives the privacy notice and signs
   the treatment consent. Special-category processing is explained in plain language,
   see [Patient Data Protection Policy](patient-data-protection-policy.md).
4. **Baseline observations.** Blood pressure, pulse, temperature, weight, and oxygen
   saturation where indicated. Recorded in the clinical record, never on paper first.
5. **History.** Presenting complaint, current medication, allergies, chronic conditions,
   pregnancy status where relevant. Allergies are entered as structured data, not free
   text; a free-text allergy is invisible to the prescribing check.
6. **Triage.** Red flags escalate immediately to the duty clinician: chest pain,
   suspected stroke, breathlessness at rest, sepsis signs, suicidal ideation.
   Escalation does not wait for the paperwork.
7. **Hand over.** Room number and expected wait told to the patient before they sit down.

## Documentation

The template is [patient-intake-form.md](file:Clinical/patient-intake-form.md). Every
field entered by reception is attributable; nobody enters clinical data under another
person's login. Escalations and rota questions are raised at the
[Clinical huddle](event:Clinical huddle) the next morning.

## Common failure modes

- Two patients with the same name. Always confirm date of birth aloud.
- Allergy captured in the free-text notes and missed by prescribing.
- Interpreter needed but not booked. Ask at booking, not at the door.
- Accompanying relative answering for a competent adult patient. Address the patient.
