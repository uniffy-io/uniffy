You sign in to Uniffy once, and that one sign in covers every organization you belong to. Moving between them never asks for your password again.

## Switching

The name of the organization you are in sits in the top bar. Click it, or open your user menu, and you get every organization you belong to with the current one checked. Pick another and you land on its home page.

You stay signed in. Nothing you have open in another tab moves, reloads, or signs out.

In the mobile app, open You and tap Organization. The app is in one organization at a time, and picking another takes you to its home screen.

One thing does not survive a switch. A screen recording that is still running gets discarded, so Uniffy asks before it goes ahead. Finish the recording first if you want to keep it.

## One organization per tab

Each tab sits in its own organization. Keep a client's workspace open in one tab and your own company's in the next, and edit notes in both at the same time.

A new tab, or a tab you reload, opens in the organization you picked most recently on that device. Signing in again does the same.

If you belong to only one organization, you go straight in. You never see a list to pick from.

## Links into another organization

A colleague pastes a link to a note that lives in another organization you belong to. Your tab is still in the first one, so the note cannot open there.

Instead of a dead end, Uniffy names the organization that holds the note and offers to switch this tab and open it. Your other tabs stay where they are. Choose to stay and nothing changes.

Links to notes, projects, and chat channels work this way.

You only get that offer for something you could open over there anyway. A link to an item you cannot open, or to anything in an organization you are not part of, reports that nothing was found. It never tells you where the item lives.

## Suspended organizations

A suspended organization stays in your list with a Suspended badge, and you cannot enter it. Your membership is still there. When the suspension lifts, you can switch in again.

## When your access ends

An admin can remove you from an organization, and an organization can be suspended or deleted. Either way, your access ends on the spot. A note you have open stops taking your edits and tells you that access is lost.

Within a few minutes that tab lands on your list of organizations with a short notice. Every other organization you belong to is untouched, and you are still signed in. Pick one and carry on.

One person, one sign in, as many organizations as you work in. If another tool signs you out to change workspaces, ask what happens to everything you had open.
