You sign in to Uniffy once, and that one sign in covers every organization you belong to. Moving between them never asks for your password again.

## Joining from an invitation

An admin invites you by email, and you join when you accept. The mail carries a link, and what it does depends on where you stand.

New to Uniffy? The link opens a short form. Pick a username and a password, and you land in the organization signed in.

Have an account but signed out? The link asks you to sign in first, then brings you back to the same invitation.

Signed in with the invited address? The link shows the organization and the role you get. Choose Join and you are in.

Signed in with another address? The link says so and offers to sign you out. An invitation only works for the address it was sent to.

Your welcome page lists every pending invitation sent to your address, each with Accept and Decline, and your user menu shows how many are waiting. Invitations appear there once your address is confirmed through an invitation link, so until then use the link in the mail. Decline takes it off your list, and the admin sees it as revoked.

An invitation lasts 30 days. If yours ran out, ask the admin to send it again.

In the mobile app, open You and tap Organization. Pending invitations sit at the top of that list. If you open an invitation in the app for an account you already have, it sends you to sign in, and you accept from that list once you are in.

## Creating an organization

Self service organization creation and public signup were developed for Uniffy's hosted SaaS. They are not intended for self hosted use. On a self hosted deployment, ask your platform operator to create an organization and invite you.

On our SaaS, the creation policy decides who may start an organization: anyone with an account, anyone who already belongs to an organization, existing owners, or only platform operators. The flow below applies when that policy permits you to create one.

When you are allowed, your welcome page offers a card to create one. View all in your user menu takes you there. When you do not see the card, ask whoever runs your Uniffy.

Give it a name. The slug, the short name in its links, follows the name until you change it. A slug that is taken is refused. That includes the slug of a deleted organization until it is purged.

You land in the new organization as its owner. It starts with a #general channel, and you invite people from the admin pages.

In the mobile app, Create organization sits at the bottom of your organization list.

## Switching

Open your user menu to see every organization you belong to with your current one checked. Pick another and you land on its home page. View all opens your welcome page, which also holds invitations waiting for you.

You stay signed in. Nothing you have open in another tab moves, reloads, or signs out.

In the mobile app, open You and tap Organization. The app is in one organization at a time, and picking another takes you to its home screen.

Switching discards a running screen recording and cancels uploads in that tab. Uniffy asks before it goes ahead. Finish recordings and uploads first if you want to keep them.

## One organization per tab

Each tab sits in its own organization. Keep a client's workspace open in one tab and your own company's in the next, and edit notes in both at the same time.

A tab you reload stays in its organization. A fresh tab opens in the organization you picked most recently on that device. Signing in again does the same.

If you belong to only one organization, you go straight in and skip the welcome page.

## Links into another organization

A colleague pastes a link to a note that lives in another organization you belong to. Your tab is still in the first one, so the note cannot open there.

Instead of a dead end, Uniffy names the organization that holds the note and offers to switch this tab and open it. Your other tabs stay where they are. Choose to stay and nothing changes.

Links to notes and projects work this way.

You only get that offer for something you could open over there anyway. A link to an item you cannot open, or to anything in an organization you are not part of, reports that nothing was found. It never tells you where the item lives.

## Leaving an organization

Leave from Settings, in the Organization section. Your access ends at once and the tab lands on your welcome page. Other tabs in that organization lose access too. Your account stays, and so does every other organization you belong to.

What you made stays with the organization. If an admin invites you back and you accept, you return to your own work.

The owner cannot leave. Hand the organization to another member first, or delete it. Both live in Organization settings in the admin pages. After a transfer you are an admin, and you can leave like anyone else.

## Suspended organizations

A suspended organization stays in your list with a Suspended badge, and you cannot enter it. Your membership is still there. When the suspension lifts, you can switch in again.

## When your access ends

An admin can remove you from an organization, and an organization can be suspended or deleted. Either way, your access ends on the spot. A note you have open stops taking your edits and tells you that access is lost.

Within a few minutes that tab lands on your welcome page with a short notice. Every other organization you belong to is untouched, and you are still signed in. Pick one and carry on.

One person, one sign in, as many organizations as you work in. If another tool signs you out to change workspaces, ask what happens to everything you had open.
