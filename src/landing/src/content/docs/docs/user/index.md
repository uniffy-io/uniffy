## What is here

[Sharing and privacy](/docs/user/sharing/) covers the rule everything else rests on. Your work is private until you share it, and there is no admin who quietly reads it anyway.

[People, teams and the org chart](/docs/user/people/) covers who is who, how profiles are filled in, and the difference between a team and a sharing group.

[Agents](/docs/user/agents/) covers working with AI in Uniffy. What an agent can do for you, and why it can never read anything you cannot.
